How it works. Files are encrypted with AES-256 in your browser (WinZip AE-2: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA1, 1000 iterations, 16-byte salt, HMAC-SHA1 authentication). Nothing is uploaded and the password is never stored — if you lose it, the archive cannot be recovered. The weak legacy ZipCrypto cipher is not used.
